### Category 5: PENALTY & INTEREST CHARGES

#### Interest on Arrears

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Rate | 21% per annum |
| Calculation | Simple Interest |
| Applied On | Previous month outstanding |
| Billing Frequency | Monthly |

**Formula:**
```
Monthly Interest = (Outstanding Amount × 21%) / 12
                 = Outstanding Amount × 1.75%
```

**Example (B202 - Santosh Parse):**
```
Previous Balance: ₹1,920
Interest Rate: 21% p.a. = 1.75% monthly
Interest: ₹1,920 × 1.75% = ₹33.60 ≈ ₹34
```

#### Penalty Charges

- Amount varies based on society decision
- Currently ₹0 for most members
